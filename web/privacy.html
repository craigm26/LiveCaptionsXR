🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we protect your data.

Last updated: February 2026

✅ Privacy-First Design

🔒 Local Processing — Audio processing happens entirely on your device for maximum privacy.
🚫 No Data Collection — We don't collect, store, or transmit your personal conversations.
📱 Device-Only Storage — All user preferences and settings are stored locally on your device.
🔓 Open Source — Our code is publicly available for transparency and security auditing.

Introduction

Live Captions XR is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our open-source accessibility application handles your information.

Information We Don't Collect

Live Captions XR is designed with privacy as a core principle:

  • We do not collect or store audio recordings
  • We do not track your conversations
  • We do not collect personal identification information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services
  • We do not share data with third parties

Local Data Processing

All audio processing and speech recognition occurs entirely on your device using platform-specific speech recognition frameworks. This means:

  • Your voice data never leaves your device
  • No internet connection is required for core functionality
  • Speech recognition uses platform-native frameworks (Apple Speech Recognition on iOS, whisper_ggml on Android)
  • Captions are generated in real-time on your device

Device Permissions

Live Captions XR requires certain permissions to function:

  • Microphone Access: Required to capture audio for real-time transcription
  • Camera Access: Required for AR functionality and spatial audio mapping
  • Speech Recognition: Uses platform-specific frameworks (Apple Speech Recognition on iOS, whisper_ggml on Android)

These permissions are used solely for the app's core functionality and are processed locally on your device.

Data Storage

The app may store the following data locally on your device:

  • User preferences and settings
  • Language and accessibility configurations
  • App usage statistics (stored locally only)

This data is never transmitted off your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

Open Source Transparency

Live Captions XR is an open-source project. Our complete source code is available on GitHub, allowing anyone to:

  • Review our privacy practices
  • Verify that we don't collect data
  • Contribute to the project
  • Report security issues

Third-Party Services

Live Captions XR uses minimal third-party services:

  • Platform Speech Recognition: Processes audio locally on device
  • ARKit: Provides augmented reality features locally
  • Core ML: Runs machine learning models locally

All processing occurs on-device without data transmission.

Children's Privacy

Live Captions XR does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we don't collect personal data from any users, this extends to users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and in our GitHub repository. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy:

  • Create an issue on our GitHub repository
  • Review our open-source code for transparency
  • Contact the development team through GitHub
